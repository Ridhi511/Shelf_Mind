# Shelf_Minds — Product Document

## Overview

Shelf_Minds is an AI-powered commerce intelligence platform designed to help Shopify merchants understand how AI shopping systems perceive and evaluate their stores.

As AI-driven commerce evolves, platforms like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity are becoming a major layer for product discovery and recommendations. Shelf_Minds helps merchants optimize their storefronts for this future.

The platform simulates multiple AI buyer personas and generates structured insights related to pricing clarity, customer trust, sustainability messaging, and overall AI recommendation readiness.

---

# Problem Statement

Most ecommerce stores today are optimized only for human customers through SEO, UI design, and digital marketing.

However, AI recommendation systems evaluate stores differently. They analyze factors such as:

- trust signals
- policy clarity
- value perception
- sustainability messaging
- transparency
- customer confidence

Currently, merchants have no visibility into how AI systems interpret or rank their stores.

Shelf_Minds solves this problem by providing AI-native storefront analysis and optimization insights.

# Core Product Philosophy

Many AI tools today are simple wrappers around language models.

Shelf_Minds was intentionally designed differently.

Instead of sending a URL into a single generic prompt, the platform uses:

- structured store scraping
- specialized AI agents
- weighted scoring systems
- analytics visualization
- recommendation prioritization
- AI-generated fixes

This creates a more believable, modular, and scalable AI commerce intelligence system.

# Key Features

## Multi-Agent AI Analysis

Shelf_Minds uses multiple specialized AI agents:

### Budget Agent
Evaluates:
- pricing clarity
- affordability
- discounts
- value perception
- premium positioning

### Trust Agent
Evaluates:
- reviews
- transparency
- guarantees
- shipping confidence
- customer trust signals

### Eco Agent
Evaluates:
- sustainability messaging
- environmental branding
- eco claims
- ethical positioning

Each agent independently generates:
- verdicts
- confidence scores
- strengths
- weaknesses

---

A weighted AI-readiness score out of 100 representing how effectively a store communicates with AI shopping systems.

## Radar Analytics Dashboard

Visual representation of:
- Trust
- Pricing
- Sustainability
- Transparency
- Product Clarity

---

## Priority Recommendation Engine

AI-generated recommendations including:
- issue

- priority level

## AI Fix Generator

Generates improved AI-optimized messaging for weak sections of a store.

Example:

Original:
“Fast shipping available.”

Improved:
“Enjoy reliable 2–3 day tracked shipping with real-time delivery updates and hassle-free returns.”

# Product Decisions

## Why Multi-Agent Architecture?

We intentionally used multiple specialized AI agents instead of one generic AI prompt because:

- outputs become more structured
- analysis becomes more believable
- future scalability improves
- results feel enterprise-grade

---

## Why Structured Analytics?

We focused on visual dashboards and confidence scoring instead of plain AI text outputs because merchants need interpretable and actionable insights.

---

## Why Actionable Fixes?

Many AI analysis platforms only identify problems.

Shelf_Minds also proposes improvements merchants can directly implement.

This increases real-world usefulness and product value.

---

# Tradeoffs

## Prioritized

- polished UX
- AI architecture
- actionable insights
- deployment-ready experience
- structured outputs

## Deprioritized

- authentication
- persistent database
- Shopify OAuth
- historical analytics
- user accounts

These were intentionally excluded to maximize core product quality within hackathon constraints.
